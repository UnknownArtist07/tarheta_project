ABOUT TARHETA

Your identity, on one card.

tarheta (from "tarjeta," calling card) is a digital calling card and link-in-bio hub built for students, professionals, and organizations across the Philippines. Instead of handing out paper business cards or juggling a dozen different links, you get one shareable URL that carries everything people need to know about you.

◆

Why we built it

Paper calling cards get lost, and a bio full of links is messy. We wanted one clean, tappable place — your name, your contacts, your work — that updates instantly and never runs out.

◆

Who it's for

Students building a portfolio, org officers sharing event links, freelancers sending one URL instead of five. If you introduce yourself often, tarheta is for you.

To give every student and professional a free, permanent digital identity — one link that's easy to share, easy to update, and always current, no matter how many times your contact details change.
When you sign up, you get a personal URL at tarheta.ph/yourname. From your hub you can build a flippable digital calling card and add unlimited cards — links, photos, or videos — that visitors can tap to open.
Yes. The core hub — your calling card and your first set of cards — is free for everyone. We may introduce optional paid customizations later, but a working hub will always be free to create.
We're actively building analytics for your hub, team/organization pages, and printable QR codes for your calling card — so your one link works just as well on a poster as it does in a text message.
